Pac-Man

Welcome to Pac-Man! Your objective is to avoid the ghosts and clear all the pac dots from the maze. You can eat power pellets to temporarily scare the ghosts and eat them for extra points.

Controls:

  • Arrow Keys (up/down/left/right): Move Pac-Man
  • 'P': Pause or Resume the Game

Objective:

  • Eat all the dots to win the game. Try and get the highest score!
  • If a ghost catches you, you lose!
